# Md. Anamul Hossain Chowdhury

Civil Engineer | Environmental Researcher

Teaching Assistant, Sylhet Engineering College | Assistant Director, UniV Research and Development Unit | University Research Assistant, Sylhet Agricultural University

I work at the intersection of civil engineering and machine learning: using ML and water quality indices (WQI, WPI) to assess groundwater pollution, air pollution, flood risk, and coastal hazards under climate change, and to support sustainable water resource management in Bangladesh.

## Education

B.Sc. (Engineering) in Civil Engineering, Shahjalal University of Science and Technology (2020-2025), CGPA 3.77/4.00 with Distinction

## Research Interests

- Water quality assessment and pollution modeling (WQI, WPI)
- Flood hazard classification and early warning systems
- Groundwater quality evaluation
- Air quality prediction
- Climate change and coastal hazards
